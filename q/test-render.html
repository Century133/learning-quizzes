Person perception

How we form impressions of other people, often within milliseconds.

Key terms

  • Person perception - the process of forming impressions and judgements about other people.
  • Snap judgement - a rapid, automatic assessment made before deliberate thought.

Names and studies

  • Ross, Amabile and Steinmetz - the quiz-show study; observers rated questioners as more knowledgeable, ignoring that roles were assigned by chance.

Attribution

Deciding why someone behaved as they did.

Key terms

  • Fundamental attribution error - over-weighting a person's character and under-weighting their situation.
  • Self-serving bias - taking credit for successes and blaming the situation for failures.
